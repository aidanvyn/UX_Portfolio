Steer App Design

Check out a functional demo of the app below!

Summary

Steer is a mobile app, focused on getting its users off their phones and focused on their goals. The app runs on the idea of basing your decisions on your 'future self'. When the user tries to open a distracting app, instead of just blocking it, it reframes distraction as a decision, not a restriction. They get a short message from their future self: “You said you wanted to finish your portfolio today”, giving them the option to proceed (but consciously), or 'Steer' to a better action (study, workout, etc).
